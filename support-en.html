Does it work offline?

The installed planner and its in-app help work offline. Opening these public web pages and sending email require an internet connection.

How can I keep my records?

Export a JSON copy in data settings before reinstalling or changing installation type. Keep it safe. Check the import summary and save the current copy before replacing data.

Why is my reminder late?

Check notification permission and battery settings. Android can delay reminders; after force-stop you may need to reopen the app. It is not an exact-minute alarm.

Do I need to pay again for Silver?

Silver is included in the planned one-time app purchase. The current private test asks for no payment. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

How do I delete or request information?

Use data settings for local records. For support messages and privacy requests, email the address above. We do not have remote access to your phone.

Support retention

Ordinary correspondence is kept while the issue is open and for at most 90 days after closure, with weekly deadline checks. Unnecessary attachments are deleted earlier. A documented legal obligation or concrete dispute may justify a limited relevant record with a reason and review date. Provider backup deletion follows its own processes.
