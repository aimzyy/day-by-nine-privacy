Privacy policy

This notice describes the current Android test app, optional email support and these privacy pages. The app does not take payments. A paid Google Play test or release requires a separate pricing decision and a review of purchase-data handling before activation.

Who is responsible?

Eriks Jarockis, an individual, operates Day by Nine under the public name Day by Nine Studio. Privacy and support contact: daybynine.support@gmail.com.

Information on your device

Your birth date and language personalise calculations and advice. Tasks, completion marks, notes, daily focus, evening ratings, weekly goals and reflections stay in this app’s storage. Advice identifiers keep selections stable. The app adds no separate encryption layer; protect your phone.

Sending and permissions

There is no app account, automatic cloud sync, advertising, analytics or sending of your notes to an AI service. We do not automatically receive planner records. Optional notifications use a local reminder; you may refuse permission. Android may delay it. The notification does not include private notes or your birth date.

Copies and deletion

JSON exports are readable, unencrypted files. You choose the file or destination through Android; a cloud destination you choose receives the copy under its own terms. Import replaces records only after confirmation. In data settings, Delete all my data clears planner records and the local pre-import copy; your language remains. Disable reminders separately in Profile. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes internal storage, but exported files and previously sent copies must be deleted separately. We cannot remotely recover unexported records.

Optional email support

We receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. Ordinary support uses legitimate interests in helping users and fixing problems; legal requests may require legal-obligation processing. Send only necessary issue details. Gmail processes correspondence under Google’s privacy policy and may process it outside your country. No support marketing or sale of messages.

Opening email passes our support address, a translated subject and the app version to your email app. Review and send the message yourself. No planner entries are attached.

Support retention

Ordinary correspondence is kept while the issue is open and for at most 90 days after closure, with weekly deadline checks. Unnecessary attachments are deleted earlier. A documented legal obligation or concrete dispute may justify a limited relevant record with a reason and review date. Provider backup deletion follows its own processes.

Your rights

Email us for applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection rights. Usually we respond within one month. You may complain to Datu valsts inspekcija.

Purchases and other services

The planned Google Play model is one purchase before download, including Silver, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Current private APK testing does not request payment. Google Play processes any store purchase under its own terms. Seller order/report access and required transaction retention will be disclosed before paid distribution. The private browser/download site is separate from this Android notice.

Visiting these privacy pages

GitHub Pages hosts these static pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. We add no analytics, contact form, cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub handles platform data under its own privacy statement; retention depends on purpose and legal duties. It may process data internationally. These pages contain no APK downloads or planner records.
